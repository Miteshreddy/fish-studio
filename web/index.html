Ready-made voices

Fish Studio has two parts. This website is just the controls. The actual voice AI runs on a free GPU in your Kaggle notebook, and the two talk over the internet using the gradio.live link.

You (this website on Vercel) │ 1. sends your script + chosen voice ▼ gradio.live link (a temporary tunnel, new every session) │ ▼ Kaggle notebook (2 × T4 GPU) • the s2-pro model reads your script • listens to the voice's reference clip and copies the voice • turns it into audio │ 2. sends the audio back ▼ You hear it here and can download it

Every time you want to use it

1Start KaggleOpen your notebook, check GPU T4 x2 and Internet are on, click Run All.
2Copy the linkAfter ~10 minutes the last cell prints https://….gradio.live.
3ConnectClick the status box at the bottom-left of this site and paste the link.
4GeneratePick a voice, paste your script, click Generate. Stop the Kaggle session when done.

Words you'll see

VoiceA short recording (10–30 s) of someone speaking. The AI copies how that person sounds. There's no training — it happens instantly each time.
Cloned voiceYour own recording plus the exact words spoken in it. Saved only in this browser.
ExpressivenessHigher sounds more emotional and varied, lower sounds calmer and steadier.
Backend linkThe address of your running Kaggle notebook. It changes every session, so you paste a new one each day.
